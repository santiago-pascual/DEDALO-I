## Methodological framework

Pre-register hypotheses, lock evaluation periods and strategy versions, audit timestamp availability and contract/session mapping, preserve costs and risk budgets, separate selection from validation, report dependence-aware uncertainty and negative findings. Compare paired session outcomes; avoid treating overlapping trades as independent. Forward evidence remains a separate test.
